1. Time Math & Deductions

Audio: "The route takes 4 hours to finish, including a 30-minute break."
Q: Time spent walking without a break?
Ans: 3.5 hours (4 hrs - 30 mins).
Trap: Selecting 4 hours.

2. Original Purpose vs. Surprise

Audio: "Built to connect habitats... Unexpectedly, it became a selfie hotspot."
Q: Which is NOT a main reason?
Ans: Selfie hotspot.
Trap: Confusing accidental popularity with the design goal.

3. Decisive Qualifiers (Mainly)

Audio: "You need to love nature, but mainly I was chosen because I'm a certified diver."
Ans: Certified diver.
Trap: Selecting basic requirements like loving nature.

4. Date Adjustments

Audio: "Is your birthday Christmas Eve?" "No, the day after Christmas Day."
Ans: 26th December.
Trap: Writing down 24th or 25th.
